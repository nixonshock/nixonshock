    How the fight works
    • While a minute is open, the tug-of-war follows price versus that minute's open. Above the open, the Monkey King's army pushes the demons back; below it, the demons push forward.
    • When the minute closes, that candle settles the round. Green candle: a demon falls. Red candle: a hero falls. Bigger candle bodies mean bigger beams, and 2.2x the usual body size or more is a multi-kill. A tiny candle is a standoff.
    • When one army is wiped out, the other wins the war and the Immortal Peaches revive everyone for the next one.
    • Every visit starts with both armies at full strength (7 vs 7). The chart and battle log still show the recent candles.
