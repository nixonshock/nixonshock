Question 1 - Qid 8533331, Scope, 2. Process, 2.2 Develop and manage project scope

Case Study: Smart Healthcare Platform Implementation Amid Regulatory and AI Changes

You are managing a project to develop a cloud-based healthcare platform for a network of hospitals and outpatient clinics. The platform will provide electronic health records, appointment scheduling, AI-assisted clinical decision support, and patient communication tools. The project uses a hybrid approach, combining predictive governance with adaptive software development.

Several releases have been completed successfully, and pilot users are generally satisfied with the solution. However, new challenges have emerged. A recently enacted healthcare privacy regulation introduces additional compliance requirements affecting data retention, audit logging, and patient consent management. The compliance team expects changes will be required, although the full impact is still being assessed.

At the same time, physicians request an AI-powered feature that summarizes patient histories before appointments. Senior executives consider the feature a competitive advantage and want it included before launch. However, it is outside the approved scope, and no additional budget or schedule has been authorized.

Stakeholders also report inconsistent communication, with some clinical representatives learning about key decisions only after implementation begins. In addition, dependencies with an external cybersecurity vendor are slipping because development documentation remains incomplete.

Meanwhile, the development team is frustrated by duplicated reporting, lengthy meetings, and conflicting priorities. Team velocity has declined over the past three iterations, and members believe administrative overhead is reducing their ability to deliver value. The project manager must balance compliance, governance, stakeholder expectations, delivery commitments, and team performance while keeping the project aligned with its business objectives.

Case Study Question 1 of 5

A senior executive requests that the AI patient-summary feature be included before the planned product launch.
